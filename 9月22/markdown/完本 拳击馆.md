## 第 1 集

**1-1海边 码头 外 日**

**人物：爱德华、索菲亚、众高管、雷克斯**

△一艘巨型顶级豪华游艇缓缓靠泊在私人码头，海浪轻拍岸堤。爱德华【霍华德集团董事长爱德华】一身深灰色高定西装，提着金属箱走下舷梯。两排高管与黑衣保镖在岸边躬身肃立。

△索菲亚【字幕：AD集团副总裁索菲亚】快步冲上前抱住爱德华。

Sophia (excited): Dad! You spent a whole year at the Paris lab… I've been going crazy missing you!

Edward (patting Sophia's back, smiling): All right, Sophia darling. Did you miss me, or your fiancé's medicine?

△索菲亚娇羞一笑。

Edward: All right. The clinical trials have all passed. Your fiancé's low sperm count is completely cured.

△爱德华打开金属箱，一支试剂静静卧在核心槽内。

Edward: AD Group invested two billion. There's only this one vial in the whole world. It can fully restore his male function and make him a real man again.

△索菲亚看着试剂，眼眶泛红。

Sophia (excited): Lucas has been tormented to the brink by this illness—he'll be so grateful to you! Dad, he's an invincible champion in the octagon, and in private he's so gentle and gentlemanly. He's at the gym right now. Let's hurry over!

△爱德华微笑着点头。

1-2日 内 拳击馆 - 豪华休息室

人物：卢卡斯、艾米莉

△艾米莉【字幕：拳击馆合伙人艾米莉】身穿紧身运动背心，双手撑在真皮沙发靠背上。卢卡斯赤裸着上半身，搂着艾米莉的腰肢，一巴掌抽在她屁股上。

Lucas (leaning in): At least you know how to serve me, unlike Sophia, that pampered princess who plays innocent all day long.

Emily (chin raised, gaze seductive): Then when are you going to dump her, Lucas?

Lucas (disgusted): If not for the tens of billions in trust she holds, and her rich father's ability to produce a life-saving vial, who would spend every day trailing after her pretending to be some devoted gentleman? It's sickening!

△卢卡斯眼底闪过一丝野心，拉过艾米莉热吻。

Lucas: Once Edward signs the prenuptial agreement, the entire AD Group is ours!

Emily (smug): Then once the assets are in hand, you have to buy me a mansion. I want one even fancier than Sophia's!

Lucas: Don't worry. The moment the group is mine, I'll dump her. (caressing Emily) Right now the important thing is to serve me well. Sophia could come by at any time.

△卢卡斯在艾米莉嘴唇上咬了一口，两个人愈发激烈。

1-3日 外 拳击馆 - 门口

人物：索菲亚、爱德华、大鲍勃

△一列豪车停在路边，首车上，索菲亚与爱德华下车。突然，索菲亚的手机急促震动。索菲亚扫了一眼屏幕，脸色沉下来。

Sophia: Father, the board called a sudden audit. All our overseas primary accounts have been frozen!

Edward: Go handle the company first, Sophia. I'll go in and see for myself what this young man is really like in private.

△索菲亚抱了抱爱德华，吻了一下他的脸颊。

△侧门处，大鲍勃正挤在门框边啃着双层汉堡。抬头，正好看见索菲亚抱住爱德华的画面。Big Bob (OS): Sophia is cheating on the boss behind his back? And with an old man! I have to tell the boss right away!

△大鲍勃掏出手机，对着贴身拥抱的两人连续抓拍数张特写，发送给卢卡斯。

1-4日 内 拳击馆- 豪华休息室

人物：卢卡斯、艾米莉

△卢卡斯和艾米莉下本身被沙发挡住，一直在耸动。

Lucas: Once I deal with that old bastard Edward, the entire West Coast elite will bow to me!

△突然手机剧烈震动。卢卡斯划开屏幕，看到索菲亚在大门口拥抱爱德华的照片。

△卢卡斯愤怒拨通大鲍勃的电话。

Lucas (furious): Big Bob! Who is this old man?

Big Bob (VO): Boss! I saw it with my own eyes—Sophia was pressed against that old man, hugging and kissing him. She was the one pushing it!

Lucas (angry): That lying bitch! She uses me as arm candy and then sneaks around with some rich old man!

Big Bob (VO): Boss! That guy is about to walk into the gym. From the way he carries himself, he's coming to provoke you!

△卢卡斯愤怒挂断电话，给自己手腕绑上战术绷带。

Lucas (gritting his teeth): Nobody is going to stop me from getting Sophia's group! Today I'll cripple him with my own hands!

第 2 集

2-1日 内 拳击馆

人物：爱德华、卢卡斯、大鲍勃、艾米莉

△爱德华坐在擂台旁的皮椅上，桌上摆着两样东西：一张黑卡，以及一份高级拳击馆产权转让合同。

△爱德华整理了一下西装衣领，看了一眼手表。

Edward (OS): Sophia has finally found someone worth entrusting herself to. This gym deed and the black card can be my gift to the young man.

△突然，拳击馆办公室的门被一脚踹开。

△卢卡斯赤裸着上身，露出一身肌肉。

△艾米莉抱臂跟在后面，冷笑着斜视全场。

△大鲍勃迈着沉重的步子跨进大门，庞大的身躯直接横在出口前，将出路完全堵死。

△爱德华站起身，神情从容地看向来势汹汹的卢卡斯。

Edward: Who are you people?

Big Bob: Boss, it's him!

Lucas (glaring viciously at Edward): You dare steal my woman? Die, old man!

△卢卡斯愤怒上前，一记右勾拳，狠狠砸爱德华侧脸上，爱德华瘫软在地上。

Edward (in disbelief): You dare hit me? Do you know who I am?

△卢卡斯直接骑在倒地的爱德华胸口上，左右开弓，连续两记重拳打在他左右脸颊，最后一个下勾拳直接将爱德华打的鼻血直流。

Lucas (angry): Of course I know! You're the old man seducing my fiancée! (looking over Edward's clothes) Spending my fiancée's money—you're not even worth that!

Edward: You've got the wrong man! There's no one in this world worth spending money to seduce!

Lucas (roaring with laughter): Old bastard. Facing death and still talking tough!

Big Bob: Do you realize the woman you seduced is our boss's fiancée!

△卢卡斯冷笑一声，一把揪住爱德华的领口，猛地将爱德华整个人拖拽起来，顺势一记飞膝，狠狠顶在爱德华的腹部，爱德华后背砸在弹性绳圈上，剧烈反弹回地面，咳出一口鲜血。

Edward: On the West Coast, no one has dared lay hands on me like this. Tell me—who are you exactly!

△卢卡斯不可一世地扬起下巴。

Lucas (arrogant): Listen up, old piece of trash! I'm Sophia's fiancé, the undefeated champion of the West Coast octagon, Lucas! And the future heir of AD Group!

Edward (surprised): What? You're Sophia's fiancé?

第 3 集

3-1日 内 拳击馆 - VIP八角笼擂台区

人物：爱德华、卢卡斯、艾米莉、大鲍勃

△爱德华捂着胸口站起身，看向卢卡斯。

Edward (OS): Sophia said her fiancé was an impeccable gentleman. This is clearly an unreasonable thug!

Edward: You're Lucas?

Lucas (sneering): So you know who I am, and you still dared come to my turf to seduce Sophia!

Edward: This is a complete misunderstanding!

△大鲍勃大步上前，将手机屏幕推到爱德华面前。

Big Bob: A misunderstanding? I saw with my own eyes how tightly Sophia hugged you at the gate!

Edward: Listen, Lucas, I'm Sophia's—

Emily (interrupting, pointing at the table): Lucas! Look at what's on the table!

△卢卡斯回头，艾米莉正指着桌上的黑卡和合同。

Emily: Sophia gave all of this to this stray man!

△卢卡斯大步走上前，抓起桌上的东西扫视。

Lucas: An unlimited black card… and the gym ownership transfer contract!

△卢卡斯一把将转让合同撕得粉碎，将桌上的所有东西扫翻在地，包括那个装着药剂的箱子。

△爱德华迅速上前护住金属箱。

Lucas (teeth clenched): Sophia has been with me a whole half year and won't even let me touch her! Then she turns around and gives an old thing like you a black card and a gym! Just how do you service her in bed!

Edward: Shut your mouth! I never imagined anyone could be so shameless!

△卢卡斯愤怒抓住爱德华的衣领。

Lucas: Old thing! Stop pretending to be a respectable man here! Once I officially take over AD Group, the whole West Coast is mine! (looking down at the case in Edward's arms) What's in there?

Emily: It must be something valuable Sophia gave him!

△爱德华死死抱住，卢卡斯一脚踹开爱德华，将箱子夺了过去。

Edward (shouting): Don't touch what's inside!

Lucas (sneering): If my fiancée bought it, it's mine! Get lost!

△卢卡斯一拳打破箱子，拿出里面的真空试剂。

Lucas: What's this? Some aphrodisiac you bought on the black market, old thing? I'll destroy it right in front of you!

Edward: Put it down, Lucas! Or you will regret it!

Lucas (sneering): Regret? I'm the undefeated fighting champion! The future heir of AD Group! In this world, no one can make me regret anything!

△卢卡斯扬手将试剂狠狠砸在地上。

△玻璃管瞬间碎裂，药液溅落一地。

△爱德华扑上前，伸手去抓地上的碎玻璃。

△卢卡斯、艾米莉和大鲍勃大笑起来。

Edward (murmuring to himself): A year of the lab's work… two billion dollars… the only vial in the world that could cure your illness—you've destroyed it all…

△卢卡斯上前，一脚踩在爱德华抓着玻璃的手背上，用力碾压。

△玻璃碎片扎进爱德华的手掌，鲜血流出。

Lucas: Now do you know what happens when you cross me?

△爱德华抬起头，盯着卢卡斯。

Edward: You have no idea what you just crushed under your own foot!

Lucas: What, a bottle of aphrodisiac?

Edward: That was the only vial in the entire world that could fully restore your male function and cure your disease!

Lucas (face changing): Impossible… Sophia said that vial is in her father's hands—Edward, the chairman of AD…

Edward (cutting him off sharply): I am Edward!!

Everyone (shocked): What!

第 4 集

4-1日 内 拳击馆

人物：爱德华、卢卡斯、艾米莉、大鲍勃

△大鲍勃后退到卢卡斯身边。

Big Bob (lowering his voice): Boss, if he really is the chairman of AD Group… we're finished!

Lucas (looking at Emily): Emily, didn't you work at AD before? Haven't you met the chairman?

Emily: You and I only took over the gym in the past half year. By then the chairman had long gone to the Paris lab… and he's extremely low-profile. There isn't even one clear photo of his face in the media!

△艾米莉突然嗤笑一声。

Emily (snorting): Lucas, you're not really scared by this old thing, are you?

Lucas: What do you mean?

Emily (smug): Sophia is twenty-four this year. Even if her father were young, he'd still be a middle-aged tycoon in his fifties. Look at this old thing—does anything about him look like the richest man?

Big Bob (nodding): Exactly! With a group as big as AD, how could the chairman come to a gym without even an attendant? He must be afraid of a beating, making up lies to scare us!

△爱德华看着地上的碎玻璃，眼神失望。

Edward: For that vial, a top research team went a year without sleep. It could not only cure your condition, but also bring hope to patients around the world!

Lucas (expression shifting): How does he know about my condition… Could this really be the treatment?

Emily: Lucas! If he dared seduce Sophia, he must have dug up everything about you long ago! This is definitely some aphrodisiac he bought on the black market, just for show!

Lucas (face darkening): Right! I almost let this old fraud bluff me! The real vial is in Edward's hands!

△卢卡斯抓着椅子，阴狠朝爱德华走去。

Lucas (teeth clenched): Old fraud. To live off Sophia's money, you must put a lot of effort into bed, huh?

Edward: Stop this foolishness. For Sophia's sake, I'll give you one last chance.

Lucas (angry): Facing death and still putting on airs!

△卢卡斯直接单手拎起椅子，双手用力扣在爱德华头上，爱德华头破血流，椅子破洞卡在他脖子上。

Edward (groaning in pain): Ugh!

△掉在地上的手机屏幕突然亮起，急促震动。

△手机屏幕特写：来电人 - 宝贝女儿索菲亚。

△电话自动接通扩音。

Edward (shouting through clenched teeth): Sophia!

Sophia (VO, anxious): Dad!

△众人彻底愣住。

人物：爱德华、卢卡斯、大鲍勃、艾米莉

△爱德华想捡起手机，卢卡斯猛地抬脚，重重一脚踩在手机屏幕上，手机彻底裂开。

Lucas (sneering, stepping forward): Want Sophia to come save you? Give up! On my turf, no one can save you!

△爱德华看着地上的手机碎片，眼神绝望。

人物：索菲亚、雷克斯

△索菲亚听着电话里的急促忙音，眉头紧锁。雷克斯快步走进办公室。

Rex: Miss, the headquarters' core accounts are already being audited.

Sophia: My father's call cut off suddenly. Could something have happened?

△索菲亚立刻拨打卢卡斯的号码，听筒里传来长时间的无人接听提示音。

Sophia (expression changing): Lucas isn't answering either… Something has definitely happened at the gym!

△索菲亚猛地站起身。

Sophia: Rex! Take people to the gym right now! Whatever happens, my father must not be harmed!

Rex: Understood!

5-3日 内 拳击馆

人物：爱德华、卢卡斯、大鲍勃、艾米莉、小弟

△大鲍勃看着地上的黑卡，一脸贪心。

Big Bob (greedy): Boss, is that black card real? I heard you can charge unlimited amounts on it.

Lucas (furious): Sophia spends this much on an old man, and I never got anything close!

△卢卡斯甩了甩手腕，一把抓起爱德华的头发，将他拎起来。

Lucas: Think a bit of filthy money lets you come to my turf and play respectable? Since you're here, I'll teach you the rules of the ring!

△卢卡斯直接将爱德华丢进八角笼里，用脚一带，门重重关紧。

Edward: Lucas, what are you trying to do?

△卢卡斯戴好拳头，活动了一下脖子，一记左勾拳重重砸在爱德华的腹部，爱德华闷哼一声，整个人撞在八角笼铁丝网上，卢卡斯贴身压上，左右摆拳连续重击爱德华的头部和肋骨。

△连续几记重拳狠狠砸下，爱德华嘴角鲜血流淌，死死咬住牙关，抬起双臂做格挡。

Edward (weakly): Stop. Stopping now is the last way out you'll leave yourself.

Lucas (leering grin): Still talking!

△卢卡斯一个侧踢，用力砸在爱德华脑袋上，爱德华摇摇欲坠，快要倒在地上。

△卢卡斯瞬间跟上，在爱德华快要倒地的瞬间，一个过肩摔，将爱德华摔在地上。

△卢卡斯用嘴咬开拳套的魔术贴，将拳套丢在一边，一只手拽着爱德华的脑袋，右手握拳，一拳又一拳地打在爱德华脸上。

Edward (weakly): You devil! I'll make you regret everything you've done today!

Lucas (angry): Is that so! Too bad you have no future!

△卢卡斯蓄力挥拳，正准备砸下去的时候。

Underling (VO, shouting): Boss, wait! Look at this!

△擂台下方，小弟举起名片，上面正是爱德华的名字。

Emily (reading in shock): Edward, Chairman of AD Group. He—he really is the chairman!

△卢卡斯愣住。

Lucas: What!

第 6 集

Edward (terrified): No!

Rex (shocked): What are you doing!

第 7 集

Lucas (cold): Never seen him. This is a training gym, not a retirement club.

△卢卡斯挣开雷克斯的一只手，膝行到索菲亚面前，抬头看她。

Lucas (pleading): Sophia, I've made mistakes, but I really do love you!

△索菲亚站在原地，眼睛发红，没有回应。

Lucas (urgent): Those words were just a moment of stupidity. I can make it up to you. From now on I'll be good to you and only you!

△爱德华看着他，缓缓摇头。

Edward (mocking): Even now, you can still say that word.

△卢卡斯转头看向爱德华，又重新抓向索菲亚的手。

Lucas (panicking): Have you forgotten how good we used to be? I risked my life for you!

Sophia (icily): Stop using feelings as an excuse for yourself.

△卢卡斯看见保镖靠近，立刻提高声音。

Lucas (shouting): I saved your life! When that car came at you, I pushed you out of the way!

△索菲亚手指微微一颤，爱德华看向女儿。

△大鲍勃跪在一旁，突然抬头，挣扎着往前挪。

Big Bob (panicking): Chairman! I have something to say. I don't want to carry his crimes anymore!

△卢卡斯脸色骤变，猛地转身。

Lucas (furious): Shut up! Say one wrong word and see what happens!

△雷克斯伸手挡住卢卡斯。大鲍勃抬起头，额头满是冷汗。

Big Bob (urgent): That car that nearly hit Miss Sophia back then—I was the one driving it!

△索菲亚身体一震，目光从卢卡斯转向大鲍勃。

Sophia (shocked): What did you say?

Big Bob (trembling): Lucas told me to wait at the intersection. He stood nearby ahead of time, just waiting to rush out and save you. He arranged all of it!

43-2 日 外 街道路口

人物：索菲亚、卢卡斯、大鲍勃

△同一条街道。索菲亚走向路口，大鲍勃坐在汽车驾驶座，看向站在路边的卢卡斯。

△卢卡斯朝他抬了抬手。汽车冲出，索菲亚僵在原地。卢卡斯扑上前，将她推倒在人行道上。

△索菲亚抬头，满眼感激。卢卡斯扶住她的肩，视线却越过她，看向已经驶离的汽车。

【闪回结束】

43-3 日 内 宴会厅

人物：索菲亚、卢卡斯、爱德华、雷克斯、大鲍勃、宾客若干

△索菲亚后退一步，眼泪缓缓落下。卢卡斯伸手想碰她，被她避开。

Sophia (choking): It was because of that that I started to trust you.

Sophia (trembling voice): So even the time you saved my life was fake?

第 44 集

44-1 夜 内 宴会厅

人物：爱德华、索菲亚、卢卡斯、雷克斯、艾米莉、大鲍勃、小弟、医生、警员、保镖、宾客若干

△大鲍勃跪在红毯上，抬头看了一眼卢卡斯，又迅速低下。

Big Bob (trembling): He knew you were the Howard family's heir, that's why he had me play along. He said once he married you, he'd never have to earn money fighting again.

Lucas (furious): Shut your mouth!

△索菲亚抬手擦掉眼泪，直视卢卡斯。

Sophia (icily): Is what he said true?

△卢卡斯避开她的目光，嘴唇不停抖动。索菲亚没有催促，始终看着他。

Lucas (low voice): At the beginning… it really was for the money.

△索菲亚闭上眼，慢慢吸了一口气。

Lucas (urgent): But later I truly fell in love with you! Sophia, you can't deny the whole future because of how it started!

△索菲亚睁开眼，抽回被他拉住的手。

Sophia (coldly): Because of that rescue, I pleaded with my father for you. Now that plea is void.

△卢卡斯扑到她脚边，死死抱住她的小腿。

Lucas (pleading): I'll die without you! I did those things because I couldn't lose you!

Edward (mocking): Staging a car accident, faking a bloodline, scheming for her fortune. This is what you call love?

△卢卡斯抬头，眼里满是慌乱。

Lucas (justifying): In the end I still wanted to be with her, didn't I? Does how the child came about really matter that much?

△索菲亚弯腰，掰开他的手，站起身向后退。

Sophia (resolute): From beginning to end, the only person you've ever truly loved is yourself.

△索菲亚抽出纸巾，擦过被他抓住的手，扔进桌边的纸篓。卢卡斯伸出的手停在半空。

△雷克斯带保镖上前。艾米莉和大鲍勃慌忙爬向爱德华，小弟与医生低头站在一旁。

Emily (begging through tears): Chairman, we've all told the truth. Lucas is the mastermind. Please let us go!

Big Bob (pleading): I'm willing to testify! I was the one who brought up the car accident!

△索菲亚看着他们，声音没有起伏。

Sophia (cruelly): When you were beating my father, why didn't you think he was innocent?

△艾米莉转头指向卢卡斯。

Emily (angry): It's all your fault! You said nothing would go wrong, and you've ruined us all!

Lucas (roaring): If you hadn't sold me out, how would it have come to this!

△雷克斯伸手隔开几人，转向爱德华。

Rex (respectfully): Chairman, the police are here. The evidence has been fully compiled.

Edward (coldly): Hand it all over. Assault, unlawful detention, medical fraud, and staging the car accident—not one item omitted.

△警员走进宴会厅。医生低下头，艾米莉突然抓住爱德华的袖口，被雷克斯拉开。

Emily (desperate): We've told you everything we know! You can't do this!

Edward (icily): You spoke out because you fear the consequences, not because you know you were wrong.

△保镖将艾米莉、大鲍勃和相关帮凶交给警员。哭求声随着众人离开，逐渐消失在门外。

△卢卡斯想跟着挪向出口，被保镖挡回红毯。其余帮凶都已带走，只剩他跪在原处。

△爱德华走到卢卡斯面前，停下脚步。

Edward (coldly): Now it's time to settle the final account.

第 45 集

45-1 夜 内 宴会厅

人物：爱德华、索菲亚、卢卡斯、雷克斯、保镖、宾客若干

△卢卡斯跪在地上，身体突然剧烈发抖。他捂住胸腹，额头冒出冷汗，整个人向前栽倒。

Lucas (in pain): My illness… Sophia, save me!

△索菲亚站在父亲身旁，双手垂着，没有伸过去。

Sophia (coldly): Lucas, I won't plead for you ever again.

△卢卡斯痛苦地翻过身，朝她爬去。索菲亚退开一步，他伸出的手抓了个空。

Lucas (pleading): This time it's real… I don't want to die…

△他转向爱德华，抓住桌脚，艰难挪到爱德华面前。

Lucas (begging): Chairman, please save me! You must have more of the treatment! Give me one more vial!

Edward (calm): At the gym, I already told you. The only medicine in the world that could save you, you smashed with your own hands.

△卢卡斯抬头，眼里最后一点希望开始动摇。

Lucas (breaking down): Then make it again! AD Group is so rich. Spend another two billion and make me another vial!

△爱德华看着他，缓缓摇头。

Edward (coldly): Re-developing it would take another year. You won't live that long.

△卢卡斯双手抓住自己的头发，拼命摇头，声音越来越失控。

Lucas (hysterical): Impossible! Sophia, plead with him for me! I don't want to die!

△爱德华打开黑色丝绒礼盒，取出药剂残骸，丢到卢卡斯面前。

Edward (calm): Whatever you did with your own hands, now face it.

△卢卡斯盯着残留的药液，眼睛猛地亮了一下。他扑过去，抓起碎裂的药瓶。

Lucas (murmuring): There's still… there's still medicine on it… I can still be cured…

△卢卡斯不顾锋利的边缘，拼命舔舐玻璃上的残留药液。碎片割伤嘴角，血顺着他的下巴淌下。

△索菲亚看见这一幕，手指一紧，转开了脸。

Lucas (mumbling): This is my medicine… my gym… I'm going to be the Howard family's son-in-law…

△碎玻璃继续划伤他。他仍攥着残骸，动作却一点点慢下来，身体抽搐，手指逐渐松开。

△药瓶残骸落地。卢卡斯倒在红毯上，再没有动。

△雷克斯走上前，俯身查看，停了片刻，缓缓站起。

Rex (low): He's dead.

△爱德华合上空了的礼盒，站直身体。索菲亚走到父亲身旁，轻轻扶住他的手臂。

△宾客让出通道。父女二人在众人的注视下走向大门，没有回头。

△红毯上，冷冻保存罐的碎片和药剂残骸散落在卢卡斯身边。

（全剧终）
